Privacy

What this website keeps.

The public pages describe the pharmacy management system. Sign-in is for the PharmPear account. The session cookie is marked so scripts cannot read it. It expires after 30 minutes without use, and after 8 hours at most. Passwords are stored as a hash, not as plain text.

The contact form opens your email app and sends a message to hello@pharmpear.com. Write only your name, work email, and a request for a call. Do not include diagnosis, medication, date of birth, insurance ID, Social Security number, or medical record numbers.

The site is hosted on Render. The domain is registered at GoDaddy. Those companies process the connection logs needed to serve the pages. PharmPear does not sell information from this site.

Clinical records, if you already work with PharmPear, are handled in the pharmacy’s own systems — not through this public page.
