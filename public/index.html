About PharmPear

The pharmacy sits between the prescription and the delivery.

PharmPear is built around one job: turn a prescription into a finished, labeled order and put it on its way. Prescribers send scripts electronically or by fax. AI reads the drug, directions, quantity, and prescriber so the order can start instead of sitting in a stack.

Before the fill, we go through the prescription for drug interactions. That review supports the fill. It does not replace the prescriber. When the order is clear, we pull the medication through the wholesalers we are connected to, prepare it, and print the labels.

Delivery is part of the same business. Local orders are assigned to a driver. Orders that need to ship become a FedEx shipment, with the label printed and the package handed off at pickup.

How the work runs

From the script to a labeled order.

Each step is part of the pharmacy, not a separate service. The same order is read, checked, prepared, and sent.

Electronic and fax

E-prescriptions land in the queue. Faxed scripts are read the same way. AI pulls the details so the order can be prepared.

Interaction review

We look at the medication on the new prescription and at the patient’s other drugs. The order stays put until that check is done.

Wholesalers and labels

We are connected to the wholesalers. The medication is ordered, the order is prepared, and the labels are printed.

Drivers

Local orders are assigned to a driver so the medication leaves with a person and a stop, not a pile of notes.

FedEx

We create the FedEx shipment, print the shipping label, and hand the package off at pickup.

One pharmacy

Intake, the interaction check, the wholesaler order, the label, and the handoff all belong to PharmPear.
