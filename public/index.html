Prescriptions

Electronic and fax, read with AI.

Electronic prescriptions

E-prescriptions land in the queue. AI pulls the drug, directions, quantity, and prescriber so the order can start.

Fax prescriptions

Faxed scripts are read the same way. AI turns the page into an order instead of leaving it in a paper stack.

Wholesalers

We are connected to the wholesalers, so the medication on the prescription can be ordered and prepared for fill.

Drug interactions

We go through the prescription before it ships.

Each prescription is reviewed for drug interactions. If something conflicts, it is caught before labels are printed and before a driver or FedEx is assigned.

  • Review the medication on the new prescription
  • Look for interactions with the patient’s other drugs
  • Hold the order until that check is done

Fulfillment

Labels, a driver, or a FedEx pickup.

Prepare the order

Once the prescription is clear, we prepare the order and print the labels.

Assign a driver

Local orders go to a driver. The stop is assigned so the medication leaves with a person, not a pile of notes.

FedEx shipment and pickup

We create the FedEx shipment, print the label, and hand it off at pickup.
