About the service

One pharmacy management service for the fax, the call, and the payment.

PharmPear is a pharmacy management service and a CRM service for pharmacies. The CRM keeps the patient, the calls, and the notes together. When a prescription arrives by fax, AI reads it and puts the drug, directions, quantity, and prescriber into the order. Electronic prescriptions come into the same service.

Calls are recorded by AI. On the patient info, the service shows a summary of the whole file and the last call summary, so the next person is not guessing and there is no confusion with patients.

The service manages the workflow by assigning patients one by one. Each assignment is either for a call or for filling the prescription. It optimizes driver routes. For shipments, it uses ShipStation to get the best price for shipping labels, then prints the FedEx label, the receipt, and the medication label.

The service is connected to the payment system, so the order and the payment stay together. It is connected to wholesalers to replenish stock, and it tracks that stock from prescription usage. It also reviews prescriptions for drug interactions. That review supports the fill and does not replace the prescriber.
