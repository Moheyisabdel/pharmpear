About PharmPear

One system for the fax, the call, and the payment.

PharmPear is a pharmacy management system and a CRM service for pharmacies. The CRM keeps the patient, the calls, and the notes together. When a prescription arrives by fax, AI reads it and puts the drug, directions, quantity, and prescriber into the order. Electronic prescriptions come into the same system.

When someone calls, AI records the call and leaves notes. The note stays with the patient, so the next person on the order is not guessing and there is no confusion with patients.

The system is connected to the payment system, so the order and the payment stay together. We are connected to wholesalers to replenish stock, and we track that stock from prescription usage. It also reviews prescriptions for drug interactions, prints labels, assigns drivers, and creates FedEx shipments and pickups. The interaction review supports the fill. It does not replace the prescriber.

How the system works

Fax, calls, notes, and payment in one place.

As a CRM service for pharmacies, PharmPear keeps the patient, the prescription, the conversation, and the payment on the same order.

AI reads the fax

Faxed prescriptions are read by AI. The drug, directions, quantity, and prescriber go into the order instead of staying on a paper stack.

AI records calls

AI records the call and leaves notes on the patient. The next person sees what was said, so there is no confusion with patients.

Payment system

PharmPear is connected to the payment system. The charge stays with the order inside the pharmacy management system.

Interaction review

The system goes through the prescription for drug interactions before labels are printed. That review supports the fill and does not replace the prescriber.

Wholesalers and stock

We are connected to wholesalers to replenish stock. The system tracks stock from prescription usage, then the order is prepared and the labels are printed.

Driver or FedEx

Local orders are assigned to a driver. Other orders become a FedEx shipment and go out at pickup.
