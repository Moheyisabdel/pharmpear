Care

Built around the slow parts of specialty pharmacy.

Referral intake

New starts, transfers, and refills land in one queue with a clear status: received, waiting on notes, approved, or scheduled.

Prior authorization

We track payer requests and tell the clinic exactly which chart notes, labs, or forms are still outstanding.

Patient updates

Patients hear what stage they are in. This website never asks for diagnosis, member ID, or medication names.

Delivery

A window, not a voicemail.

Scheduled handoff

Routes are set for the day. Patients get a window, and drivers work from the next stop — not a stack of texts.

Cold and routine

Temperature-sensitive and standard deliveries stay on separate handling notes so the right stop gets the right care.

Proof it arrived

Completed stops are checked off. If something was marked by mistake, it goes back on the route.
